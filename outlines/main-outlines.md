# Main Outline

#### Episode 1
##### Plots:

1. Damien slowly builds friendships with the strangers around him.
   1. He meets an awkward friend who eventually becomes close to him.
1. Damien begins to have feelings for Dahlia, the first woman to show him affection.
1. Damien learns to play guitar amid world-ending threats.
   1. The monster causing the threat comes from a mysterious, cursed alien girl.
1. Damien joins a band to earn money and get closer to Dahlia.
   1. He pretends he is already good, but he actually spends a long time learning one song at a time.
1. Damien's past with his gang and the people he bullied is slowly revealed.
1. Damien tries to fit in with his new friends while ruining Mrs. Shiela's life.
1. Damien and Dahlia share intimate moments while their band performs at different places.
   1. From these moments, Damien learns to write songs based on his feelings and happy memories with Dahlia.
1. Damien faces punishment and learns the consequences of what he did to Mrs. Shiela.
1. Damien becomes friends with Jake after the betrayal involving Mrs. Shiela.
1. Damien falls too deeply for Dahlia only after hurting her feelings, when she is ready to move on.
   1. During PE class, Damien meets a mysterious girl who can summon magical creatures to cheat during a PE test.
1. Dahlia has a relationship with Clark, an old friend and member of Damien's gang.
1. Damien and Dahlia's mutual friend exposes Dahlia's rant about her feelings for Damien.
   1. Damien hears Dahlia's voice on a secret recording made during a girls' party.
   1. Dahlia admits that her feelings for Damien were real and that she knew his songs were about her.

##### Focus:

1. Damien's emotional growth, from isolated former bully to someone capable of friendship and love.
1. The tension between ordinary high-school life, music, and supernatural threats.

##### Themes:

1. Redemption and accountability.
1. First love and emotional vulnerability.
1. Creativity as a way to process feelings.
