# Scene: Lunch Break at Joan's House

## Context
* **Location:** Joan's House
* **Setting:** Lunch Break
* **Characters:** Damien, Joan, Elena, Mateo, Ben

## Objective
* Establish Joan's history with Dahlia (they were bandmates) and plant the idea that Dahlia might be more
  invested in Damien than she's letting on.
* Introduce the physics-song group project as the engine for the chapter's back half.

## Key Beats
* Explain the scene briefly.
    - It was lunch time.
    - Damien was outside Joans house, while others are still eating lunch.
* While eating, Elena and Joan are stalking Dahlia's social media.
    ```
        Joan: Well, I can't see any sign here that she hates Damien for what happened last time.
        Elena: But what if she's on a different account 'cause she knows we know Damien?
        Joan: She's probably gonna tell me that.
        Elena: How are you sure?
        Joan: Well, out of nowhere, on the first day of school, she was already asking me the name of
        the boy sitting in the front row.
        Elena: Really?
        Joan: Yeah, but I didn't know Damien yet that day.
        Elena: So you were close friends?
        Joan: Uhm, we're in a band actually.
        Elena: Well, Damien is so lucky to have her.
        *Elena looks outside, to see Damien looking at road*
    ```
* Damien is just outside the house, pretending he doesn't hear a thing.
    - He watches cars passing by on the road.
    - He feeds a stray dog with his leftover food.
* Mateo is scrolling on his phone when he sees Ms. Shiela's message in the group chat.
* Ms. Shiela announces a performance task for finals: a song about physics.
    ```
        Good morning class, we will be having a project for finals. You have to sing a song
        about physics — you can pick any formula, theorem, or law, just make sure to provide
        thorough explanation and examples.

        I'm giving you the freedom to pick your own groupmates, however each group must only
        have 4 members.
    ```
    - Mateo rants about it.
    ```
        Mateo: Seriously? This is a waste of time. I thought we'd be doing
        experiments with a wrecking ball like the professors on YouTube.
        Ben: *wiping down the dining table*
        Ben: It's almost 1PM, we should head to school now...
    ```
* They finish lunch.

## Outcome
* The group's project pairing is set (Damien, Mateo, Ben, Elena, Joan — need to confirm this is the group of 4/5
  and reconcile the headcount against the "4 members" rule Ms. Shiela just gave).
    - but this pairing problem would not be discussed it would be a problem for the next chapter.
* Seeds planted: Joan/Dahlia history, the group project, Damien's habit of overhearing and staying silent.

# Scene: Staring During Classes

## Context
* **Location:** Classroom
* **Characters:** Damien, Dahlia, Sir Kent, classmates

## Objective
* Use Sir Kent's lesson as a mirror for Damien's own guardedness — a teacher who used to hide a whole
  version of himself, now standing in front of a student doing the same thing.
* Land the Damien/Dahlia eye-contact beat without needing dialogue.

## Key Beats
* Sir Kent explains relationships, functions, and equations — the recurring first-week math lesson.
    ```
        I'm pretty sure you're familiar with our first lesson.
        This lesson has always been the first one of every school year in math:
        functions, equations, and relationships.
        (continues giving distinctions)

        Now, talking about relationships — who here has already had, or currently has, one?
    ```
* Sir Kent jokingly asks who's been in a relationship.
    - Everyone raises their hands except a few students, Damien included.
    - He looks back and sees Dahlia; they hold eye contact for a moment. Dahlia raises her hand. Damien keeps
      his down.
* Sir Kent reassures the class it's nothing to be embarrassed about — then, unprompted, pivots into his own
  backstory: a gang member, a college dropout.
    - **Beat to add before the reveal:** a half-second where the joking energy in the room drops — someone's
      laugh trails off, a couple of students exchange glances — so the tonal shift from "haha raise your hands"
      to "I was in a gang" doesn't happen mid-sentence with no runway.
    - **Beat to add during it:** Damien, specifically, goes still. Not because it's shocking gossip like
      everyone else — but because it's the first time an adult in his life has described *hiding a version of
      himself* out loud. Doesn't need a line of dialogue — a held reaction is enough.
    ```
        You know, before I met my wife, I was a completely different person.
        I enrolled in engineering in college, but dropped out in my 2nd year.
        I got involved in some crime, and I was too afraid to tell my parents.

        So I dropped out. But a friend helped me finish studying — a different course,
        at a different university.

        That friend is my wife now. (laughs loudly)
        God, I'm lucky I met someone so patient with me...

        Anyways — back to the topic.
    ```
* Everyone settles back into listening. Damien takes a beat longer than the rest of the class to do the same.

## Outcome
* Thematic groundwork laid: hiding parts of yourself isn't unique to Damien, and it doesn't have to stay hidden
  forever. Nothing needs to be said aloud — Damien just needs to *notice* it.

# Scene: Mateo and Ben Teasing Damien About Being Single

## Context
* **Location:** The road home
* **Setting:** Walking home after school, afternoon
* **Characters:** Damien, Mateo, Ben

## Objective
* Force Damien to almost say something honest out loud, then watch him retreat from it in real time.
* Give Damien's inner monologue room to land as the emotional center of the chapter.

## Key Beats
* Walking home, they pass a couple of sweet, obviously-together couples. Damien watches them go by.
* Mateo and Ben needle him about it.
    ```
        Mateo: Dude, it's obvious she likes you — what are you even waiting for? The end of the world?
        Ben: He's kind of right. Don't you feel anything for her? Even a little?
        Damien: I think I can...
        *Mateo and Ben slow their pace, waiting for him to finish the sentence.*
        Damien: ...I guess I can play, for Ms. Shiela's performance task.
        *Mateo looks confused.*
        Damien: The song, dude.
        Mateo: Oh — right, we forgot to ask when that's even due.
    ```
    - He doesn't actually answer the real question. The deflection is the answer — flag this in prose as
      *Damien choosing* the redirect, not stumbling into it, so it reads as guardedness rather than a
      non-sequitur.
* Damien's inner monologue, on why he took that off-ramp:
    ```
        I'm not stupid enough to not know what they're talking about.
        But the truth is, the way I see her is like a flower that somehow grew
        between the cracks in concrete.
        It's not supposed to happen.
        But it does.
        I wish I had the soil for it to grow. But how?
    ```
* Damien realizes he's alone — Mateo and Ben have already split off toward their own streets.
* He's been thinking so hard he walked the whole way home without flying, and didn't even notice.

## Outcome
* Damien ends the chapter having said something true out loud (barely, sideways, in a way only he understands
  the weight of) and immediately burying it under a joke about music.
* The "song" deflection is now a live thread: he's said it out loud to Mateo, which sets up next chapter's
  scene where Mateo repeats it publicly.
